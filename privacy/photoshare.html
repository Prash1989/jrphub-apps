Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

1. Overview

PhotoShare: Privacy Cleaner is an application developed by JRP Apps that strips EXIF metadata — including GPS coordinates, camera make and model, timestamps, and device fingerprints — from photos entirely on your device before you share them.

We believe your personal photos are yours alone. This Privacy Policy explains what information the application accesses, how it is used, and the strong privacy guarantees built into PhotoShare.

Core privacy principle: PhotoShare operates 100% on-device. Your photos are never uploaded to any server — not even JRP Apps servers. All metadata analysis and EXIF cleaning happens locally, and no photo content leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to share it yourself.
🔒 100% On-Device All processing runs locally. Zero photos are transmitted to any server.
📍 GPS Erased Latitude, longitude, altitude, and geocoding tags are completely removed.
📷 Camera Info Stripped Device make, model, lens, serial numbers, and software tags are removed.
🚫 Zero Tracking No analytics, no advertising SDKs, no telemetry in the core app.

2. Photos and Media Access

PhotoShare requests access to photos only when you explicitly choose to select them within the app. The application uses the modern Android Photo Picker (API 33+) and platform equivalents on other operating systems, which provide access to only the specific photos you select — not your entire media library.

Photo access is used solely for the following purposes:

  • Reading the EXIF and XMP metadata embedded in photos you select.
  • Displaying a preview of the original and cleaned photo within the app.
  • Writing a sanitized copy of the photo with metadata removed.
  • Saving the cleaned photo to your device gallery when you choose to do so.
  • Sharing the cleaned photo via the Android share sheet when you initiate sharing.

PhotoShare does not scan, index, or access photos beyond those you explicitly open or select within the application.

3. EXIF Metadata Processing

When you open a photo in PhotoShare, the app reads the EXIF, XMP, and IPTC metadata embedded in the image file. This metadata may include:

  • Location data: GPS latitude, longitude, altitude, direction, and geocoded place names.
  • Device information: Camera make, model, lens, firmware, and serial numbers.
  • Timestamps: Capture date and time, modification dates, and timezone information.
  • Image settings: Aperture, shutter speed, ISO, focal length, and white balance.
  • Software tags: Editing software names and version strings.

This metadata is displayed to you within the app so you can see exactly what private information your photo contains. When you trigger cleaning, PhotoShare removes the selected categories of metadata and produces a new sanitized image file.

No metadata is stored or transmitted. Metadata read from your photos is used only to display the inspection view and to perform the cleaning operation. It is never stored persistently, logged, or sent to any external service.

4. On-Device Processing

All EXIF metadata analysis and removal is performed entirely on your device. PhotoShare does not use cloud APIs, remote servers, or network connections for any photo processing.

Specifically, the following operations run locally on your device:

  • Parsing and inspecting EXIF, XMP, and IPTC metadata.
  • Generating the location map preview tile (fetched from OpenStreetMap's public tile server using only coordinates — no account or identity is involved).
  • Stripping GPS, camera, timestamp, and other metadata tags.
  • Re-encoding the cleaned image with optional JPEG quality adjustment.
  • Caching temporary cleaned files for sharing (auto-pruned after 2 hours or when the cache exceeds 50 MB).

5. Temporary Cache

PhotoShare stores sanitized photo copies in a private temporary cache directory on your device. This cache exists solely to enable the share-sheet flow after cleaning (sharing requires a local file URI).

The cache is managed as follows:

  • Cached files are automatically deleted when they are older than 2 hours.
  • The entire cache is pruned when its total size exceeds 50 MB.
  • You can clear the cache at any time from Settings → Storage & Cache.
  • The cache is cleared when you uninstall the app.

No original photos are modified or moved. Only sanitized output copies are placed in the cache. The cache is stored in the application's private storage and is not accessible to other apps.

6. Information We Do Not Collect

PhotoShare: Privacy Cleaner does not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Your photos or their contents.
  • GPS coordinates or location information extracted from your photos.
  • Device model, IMEI, or advertising identifiers for analytics purposes.
  • Usage statistics, feature interaction logs, or behavioral data.
  • Personal account information (the app requires no sign-in).
No account required. PhotoShare works entirely without creating an account, signing in, or providing any personal information.

7. Map Preview Tiles

When a photo contains GPS coordinates, PhotoShare may display a static map tile from OpenStreetMap to visually illustrate the location embedded in the photo's metadata.

This tile request is made directly from your device to OpenStreetMap's public tile servers using only the coordinates from the photo. The request includes no personally identifying information beyond what is present in any standard HTTP request (e.g., your device's IP address as seen by the tile server).

OpenStreetMap tile server usage is governed by the OpenStreetMap Foundation Privacy Policy. Once the photo is cleaned, GPS coordinates are erased and no further tile requests are made for that photo.

8. Permissions

The application requests only the permissions necessary for the features that require them:

Photos / Media (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES) Used to let you select photos for metadata inspection and cleaning via the system Photo Picker. On Android 10+ (API 29+), no broad storage permission is required.
Photos / Media (WRITE / Gallery Save) Requested only when you choose to save a cleaned photo to your gallery. Uses the MediaStore API — no broad storage permission needed on Android 10+.
Internet (INTERNET) Used only to fetch OpenStreetMap map preview tiles when a photo has GPS coordinates. Not used for any data upload or analytics.
Share Intent (receive_sharing_intent) Allows the app to receive photos shared from other apps (e.g., your camera roll or gallery). Used only to load the shared photo into the cleaner.

You can manage permissions through your device settings. Denying a permission disables only the associated feature; the core metadata inspection and cleaning workflow does not require network or gallery permissions.

9. Third-Party Services

PhotoShare uses a minimal set of open-source libraries and platform services:

  • OpenStreetMap tile servers — for map preview tiles only (see Section 7).
  • Google Play / Android platform — for app distribution, Photo Picker, and MediaStore gallery saving.
  • flutter_riverpod, native_exif, image, share_plus, image_picker, gal — open-source Flutter packages used on-device for state management, metadata processing, image handling, sharing, and gallery saving respectively.

PhotoShare does not integrate advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, crash-reporting services, or social-login SDKs in its core build.

10. Data Sharing

JRP Apps does not sell or rent your personal information or photo content.

PhotoShare does not share your photos or the metadata extracted from them with any third party, except in the following cases initiated solely by you:

  • When you tap "Share Clean", the sanitized photo is passed to the Android share sheet so you can send it to an app of your choice. The receiving app's own privacy policy governs how it handles the photo.
  • When you tap "Save to Gallery", the sanitized photo is written to your device's media library using the MediaStore API.

11. Data Storage

PhotoShare stores only the following data locally on your device:

  • App preferences — theme mode, accent color, and privacy-stripping toggles (stored in shared preferences, private to the app).
  • Temporary cache — sanitized photo copies used for sharing (automatically pruned; see Section 5).

No photo content, metadata, or location data is stored persistently by the app. All local data is private to the application and is removed when you uninstall it.

12. Children's Privacy

PhotoShare: Privacy Cleaner is not specifically directed at children under the applicable minimum age for digital services. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

13. Security

100% On-Device Processing Photos never leave your device for cleaning. There is no server-side processing that could be breached.
Private App Storage The temporary cache uses the app's private storage directory, inaccessible to other applications without explicit sharing.
Minimal Permissions The app requests only the permissions needed for each specific feature, reducing its access footprint.
No Credentials Stored No sign-in, no account, no passwords. Nothing to steal from the app layer.

Your device's own security — screen lock, encryption, and operating-system access controls — is your primary protection for photos stored on the device.

14. Your Choices

You are always in control with PhotoShare:

  • Grant or deny the Photo Picker permission from device settings.
  • Choose which metadata categories to strip (GPS, camera info, timestamps).
  • Clear the temporary cache at any time from Settings → Storage & Cache.
  • Review stripped metadata in the inspection view before cleaning.
  • Decide when and where to share or save the cleaned photo.
  • Uninstall the app to remove all local app data.
