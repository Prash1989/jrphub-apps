Photo Docs Cleaner
Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

1. Overview

Photo Docs Cleaner is an application developed by JRP Apps that helps users organize, review, clean up, and manage photos and document images stored on their device.

We respect your privacy and aim to minimize the amount of information that leaves your device. This Privacy Policy explains what information the application may access, how that information is used, and the choices available to you.

Privacy principle: Your photos and documents are personal content. Photo Docs Cleaner is designed to use such content only as necessary to provide the features you request.

2. Photos and Documents

Photo Docs Cleaner may request access to photos, videos, document images, or other media stored on your device depending on the features you use and the permissions provided by your operating system.

This access may be used to provide features such as:

  • Browsing and selecting photos or document images.
  • Identifying potential duplicates or similar media.
  • Helping you review unnecessary or unwanted files.
  • Organizing or categorizing visual content.
  • Deleting media when you explicitly choose to do so.
  • Displaying previews or other information required by the app.

The application does not obtain access to your media beyond the permissions and functionality provided by your device and the choices you make within the app.

3. On-Device Processing

Features that analyze photos or document images may process visual information on your device.

Where processing is performed locally, the relevant image data is analyzed directly on your device rather than being uploaded to a JRP Apps server for that processing.

Local processing may be used for tasks such as identifying similar images, detecting duplicates, analyzing document images, generating previews, or helping determine which files may be suitable for cleanup.

The exact processing performed depends on the version of the app and the features you use.

4. Deleting Photos and Documents

Photo Docs Cleaner may provide functionality for deleting photos, videos, or document images that you select.

Deletion is performed only as a result of an action or instruction initiated through the application or the operating system's media management mechanisms.

Depending on your device and operating-system configuration, deleted media may remain temporarily in a system-managed trash, recycle bin, or recently deleted area. You should review your device's own deletion and recovery controls before permanently removing important content.

5. Information We May Collect

The application may process limited technical or usage information when required to operate the app or when third-party services are enabled.

Depending on the app version and enabled services, this may include:

  • App version and operating-system information.
  • Device or installation-related identifiers.
  • Crash and diagnostic information.
  • Basic feature or interaction information.
  • Advertising-related information where advertising services are used.

We do not intentionally collect the contents of your personal photos or documents merely because you use the application.

6. Advertising

If Photo Docs Cleaner displays advertisements, advertising providers may process certain information required to deliver, measure, and personalize advertisements according to their own policies and the choices available through your device or applicable advertising controls.

Advertising providers may use identifiers and other technical information associated with advertising requests. JRP Apps does not control the independent privacy practices of third-party advertising providers.

Where applicable, advertising-related data handling is governed by the privacy policy and terms of the advertising provider in addition to this Privacy Policy.

7. Analytics and Crash Reporting

If analytics or crash-reporting services are included in the installed version of Photo Docs Cleaner, they may receive technical information necessary to understand application performance, diagnose crashes, and improve reliability.

Such information may include app version, operating-system version, device characteristics, error information, and diagnostic details.

These services are not intended to receive the contents of your personal photo or document library as part of ordinary crash or analytics reporting.

8. Third-Party Services

Photo Docs Cleaner may use third-party services for functions such as application distribution, advertising, diagnostics, analytics, authentication, or other technical services.

Examples may include services provided by:

  • Google Play and Android platform services.
  • Apple platform services where applicable.
  • Advertising providers where advertising is enabled.
  • Analytics or crash-reporting providers where enabled.

Third-party services operate under their own privacy policies and terms. Their processing of information is governed by those policies as applicable.

9. Permissions

The application requests only the permissions necessary for the features that require them. Depending on your device and operating system, permissions may include access to:

Photos / Media Access is used to display, analyze, organize, or clean media that you choose to manage with the application.
Notifications May be used for optional reminders, task completion information, or other app-related notifications.

You can manage available permissions through your device settings. Restricting a permission may prevent the associated application feature from functioning.

10. Data Sharing

JRP Apps does not sell or rent your personal information.

Personal information is not intentionally shared with third parties except where necessary to provide enabled services, comply with legal obligations, protect the application and its users, or operate third-party services described in this Privacy Policy.

Your photos and documents are not intended to be shared with other people by the application unless you explicitly use an operating-system or application feature that performs such sharing.

11. Data Storage

Photo Docs Cleaner may store application settings, preferences, temporary processing information, and other application data locally on your device.

Local application data remains subject to the security of your device, operating system, account, and physical access controls.

You should use appropriate device security measures such as a screen lock and operating-system security features to protect your personal information.

12. Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored locally by the application generally remains on your device until you remove it, clear the application's data, uninstall the application, or otherwise delete it using available device controls.

Some information handled by third-party services may be retained according to those providers' own retention policies.

When you delete photos or documents using Photo Docs Cleaner, the application's handling of those files follows the applicable operating-system media APIs and your device's deletion behavior.

13. Children's Privacy

Photo Docs Cleaner is not specifically directed at children under the applicable minimum age for providing personal information without parental consent.

We do not knowingly request personal information from children for purposes unrelated to providing the application's functionality.

14. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the application. Depending on the functionality and platform, these measures may include:

Local Processing Processing can occur directly on the device where supported by the feature.
Platform Security The application relies on operating-system permission and security mechanisms to control access to personal media.
Limited Data Collection The app is designed to minimize unnecessary collection of personal information.
Third-Party Controls External services are subject to their own security and privacy mechanisms.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

15. Your Choices

You can control your use of Photo Docs Cleaner by:

  • Granting or denying requested device permissions.
  • Removing photos or documents you no longer want.
  • Uninstalling the application.
  • Managing notification permissions through device settings.
  • Managing advertising and privacy settings provided by your device or applicable third-party services.

Some features may not work correctly if the permissions required for those features are denied.

16. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the application, applicable services, legal requirements, or our privacy practices.

When changes are made, the updated policy will be published on this page and the "Last updated" date will be revised.

17. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or Photo Docs Cleaner, you can contact JRP Apps through the contact information provided on our website.
