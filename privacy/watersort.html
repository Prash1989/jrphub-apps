Water Sort Master

Privacy Policy

Water Sort Master is a color sorting puzzle game with hundreds of challenging tube-sorting puzzle levels.

Last updated: August 2026
Play with complete privacy. Water Sort Master stores your puzzle progression and settings locally on your device without collecting personal data or requiring an account.

Information We Collect

Water Sort Master is a color sorting puzzle game. The app does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personally identifiable information.

Gameplay and Local Data

Your puzzle level progression, unlocked features, audio preferences, and settings are stored locally on your device only. They are not uploaded to any external server.

Permissions

The app only requests standard permissions necessary for game operation and display. It does not require access to your contacts, camera, microphone, precise location, or photos.

Advertisements and Third-Party Services

Water Sort Master may display advertisements provided by third-party ad networks such as Google AdMob. These services may collect pseudonymous identifiers and diagnostic data in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

Data Security

Because your game progress is stored locally on your device, you remain in full control of your data.

Children's Privacy

Water Sort Master is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically when new features, levels, or services are introduced.
