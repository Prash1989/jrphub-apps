Your privacy matters. HD Wallpapers does not require an account for normal wallpaper browsing and download features. The App may display advertisements to support free access.

Information We Handle

HD Wallpapers is primarily used to browse, preview, and download wallpaper images. JRP Apps does not require users to create an account for the App's normal wallpaper features.

Preferences, favorites, wallpaper metadata, cached content, download history, and other app settings may be stored locally on your device to improve the experience and support app functionality.

Wallpapers downloaded to your device may remain available outside the App until you delete those files yourself.

Wallpaper Sources and Downloads

HD Wallpapers may include wallpapers bundled directly with the application and wallpapers hosted remotely using services such as ImageKit.

When you choose to download a wallpaper, the App saves the selected image using device or operating-system functionality. Remote wallpapers require an internet connection and may generate normal network requests to the hosting provider.

HD Wallpapers may use system-provided storage, media, photo, download, file saving, or sharing functionality when you choose to download, save, open, set, or share a wallpaper.

Data Sharing

JRP Apps does not sell personal information. Limited technical information may be processed by advertising, hosting, platform, security, and infrastructure providers where necessary to provide the App and its services.

Children's Privacy

HD Wallpapers is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. Parents or guardians may contact JRP Apps with any privacy concern.

Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored app data can be removed by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App. Wallpapers downloaded outside the App must be deleted separately from the location where they were saved.

Information processed independently by advertising, hosting, or platform services is subject to those providers' own retention and deletion practices.
