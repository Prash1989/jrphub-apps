Privacy Policy

Style Text Pro is designed to help you create and use styled Unicode text while keeping the experience simple, private, and user-controlled.

Last updated: August 8, 2026
Privacy first. Style Text Pro does not require an account for its core text styling features and does not display advertising.

Information We Handle

Text entered into Style Text Pro is processed to generate Unicode-styled variations and related text outputs. JRP Apps does not require users to create an account for the App's core styling features.

Local App Data

Preferences, favorites, recent text, and selected styles may be stored locally on your device to improve the experience.

Permissions

Clipboard and sharing actions occur only when you choose to copy or share generated text. Style Text Pro does not require access to contacts, precise location, camera, or microphone for its core functionality.

Third-Party and Platform Services

The App may rely on standard platform services such as Google Play Services where required for normal Android app functionality. Any information processed independently by those services is governed by their respective privacy policies.

Advertising

Style Text Pro does not display third-party advertisements and does not use advertising features as part of its core user experience.

Data Sharing

JRP Apps does not sell personal information. Information is not shared for advertising purposes. Limited technical information may be processed by platform services only where necessary for app functionality, security, diagnostics, or legal compliance.

Children's Privacy

Style Text Pro is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. Parents or guardians may contact JRP Apps with any privacy concern.

Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored app data can be removed by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App. Information processed independently by platform services is subject to those providers' own retention and deletion practices.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the App's features, permissions, or data practices change. The latest version will remain available on this page.

Contact

Style Text Pro is developed by JRP Apps. For privacy questions, contact support@jrphub.com.
