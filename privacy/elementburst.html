Element Burst Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 23, 2026

Element Burst is developed by JRP Apps.

Overview

Element Burst is a puzzle game that may use online services for account authentication, player statistics, rankings, gameplay telemetry, advertising, diagnostics, and administrative analytics.

JRP Apps does not sell your personal information. Information collected by Element Burst is used to operate the game, maintain player progress and statistics where applicable, provide rankings, improve reliability and gameplay quality, prevent abuse, and support advertising where enabled.

Game Data Stored on Your Device

Element Burst may store game-related information locally on your device, including gameplay progress, completed levels, coins or rewards, settings, tutorial state, audio preferences, cached statistics, session information, and other data required for normal game operation.

Some locally stored information may remain on your device until you reset the game, clear the app's data through your device settings, or uninstall the app.

Account and Authentication

Element Burst may use Google Firebase Authentication to identify or authenticate players where account functionality is enabled. Depending on the sign-in method available in the app, account-related information may include a Firebase user ID, email address, display name, profile photo URL, authentication provider, account status, and sign-in timestamps.

Authentication information is used to provide account-related features and to associate supported cloud features, statistics, rankings, or player records with the correct user.

Firebase / Cloud Firestore

Element Burst may use Google Firebase and Cloud Firestore to store or synchronize limited game and account information needed for online features.

Depending on the enabled features, this may include player identifiers, profile-related information, gameplay statistics, ranking information, streaks, level-completion totals, reward or coin-related state, feature configuration, moderation or integrity metadata, and synchronization timestamps.

Player Statistics and Rankings

Element Burst may provide player-facing statistics such as daily streak, levels completed today, weekly progress, best streak, total levels completed, win rate, play days, coins or rewards earned, personal bests, rank, and percentile.

Global or weekly rankings may use a player identifier, display name or other permitted profile information together with relevant gameplay statistics. Rankings are intended to show game performance and should not expose private account credentials.

Gameplay Telemetry and Analytics

Element Burst may collect gameplay and product telemetry to understand how the game is being used and to improve reliability, balancing, engagement, and user experience.

This information may include app version, platform, session activity, levels started or completed, wins or failures, progress milestones, reward activity, ad views or completions, feature usage, error states, performance information, timestamps, and other gameplay events.

Telemetry may be processed in the background or in batches so that analytics collection does not unnecessarily interfere with gameplay responsiveness.

Administrative Analytics

JRP Apps may use restricted administrative analytics to operate and understand Element Burst. Administrative analytics are separate from the player-facing Statistics screen and are not intended to be exposed to ordinary players.

Administrative analytics may include known users or installations, daily or weekly active users, gameplay activity, levels completed, average levels completed per active user, ad views or completions, engagement trends, feature usage, technical health information, and similar aggregate or operational metrics.

Advertising

Element Burst may display advertisements through Google AdMob on supported platforms where advertising is enabled. Google Mobile Ads may process information such as device or app identifiers, IP address, general location derived from IP address, app information, ad interactions, diagnostic information, and fraud-prevention information in order to provide, measure, secure, and where permitted personalize advertising.

Advertising providers process information according to their own privacy terms and your applicable Google, device, and advertising-consent settings.

Diagnostics and Crash Information

Element Burst may process diagnostic or technical information to identify crashes, failed network operations, authentication problems, synchronization issues, performance problems, or other defects.

Diagnostic information may include app version, operating system or platform, device or runtime characteristics, timestamps, error details, network state, feature health information, and related technical data.

Network Access

Element Burst requires network access for enabled online features such as Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, telemetry, statistics, rankings, advertising, configuration, and administrative services.

Core game availability and the behavior of offline features may depend on the version of the app and whether a particular feature requires a network connection.

Permissions and Device Access

Element Burst requests only permissions or system access needed for enabled features. This may include network access and other permissions required by the operating system or supported advertising and Google services.

Permissions are used for the purposes associated with the relevant feature and are subject to the controls provided by your device and operating system.

Data Security

Element Burst uses the security mechanisms provided by the operating system, Google Firebase, and other supported service providers for information stored or transmitted through those services.

No method of electronic storage, transmission, authentication, or device protection can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You are responsible for protecting your device and any account credentials used with the game.

Data Retention and Deletion

Local game data remains on your device until it is deleted through available in-app options, cleared through device settings, reset where supported, or removed by uninstalling the app.

Cloud-hosted account, statistics, ranking, telemetry, or configuration data may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the relevant service, maintain game integrity, prevent fraud or abuse, resolve technical issues, meet legal obligations, or support legitimate operational requirements.

Where Element Burst provides an account-deletion feature, deletion requests may remove or schedule removal of user-owned account information covered by that feature. Some limited records may be retained where reasonably necessary for security, fraud-prevention, legal, billing, purchase-verification, or service-integrity purposes.

Your Choices

Depending on the version and platform, you may be able to control whether you sign in, manage available advertising privacy choices, control device permissions, or delete local or account-related information using the controls provided by Element Burst, Google, or your operating system.

Children's Privacy

Element Burst is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If JRP Apps becomes aware that personal information has been collected from a child in a manner requiring parental consent and such consent was not obtained, reasonable steps will be taken to delete the information as required.

Third-Party Services

Depending on the enabled features and platform, Element Burst may use Google services including Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Google AdMob, Google Play services, and related Firebase or Google infrastructure. These providers may process technical, authentication, account, advertising, analytics, or service-usage information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

Google privacy information: Google Privacy Policy · Firebase Privacy and Security · How Google uses information from apps that use Google services

International Data Processing

Third-party providers such as Google may process authentication, analytics, advertising, telemetry, or cloud-hosted information in countries where they or their service providers operate. Their processing is governed by their applicable terms, privacy policies, and data-protection commitments.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Element Burst adds, removes, or changes account features, statistics, rankings, telemetry, advertising, permissions, administrative analytics, or third-party services. The updated policy will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
