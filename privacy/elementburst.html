Your privacy is respected. JRP Apps does not sell your personal data. Information collected by Element Burst is used strictly to operate the game, maintain player progress, provide rankings, prevent abuse, and improve gameplay reliability.

Overview

Element Burst is developed by JRP Apps. It is a puzzle game that may use online services for account authentication, player statistics, rankings, gameplay telemetry, advertising, diagnostics, and administrative analytics.

We aim to collect only information that is reasonably necessary to deliver game features and ensure a smooth, stable experience across devices.

Game Data Stored on Your Device

Element Burst may store game-related information locally on your device, including gameplay progress, completed levels, coins or rewards, settings, tutorial state, audio preferences, cached statistics, session information, and other data required for normal game operation.

Locally stored information remains on your device until you reset the game, clear app data through your device settings, or uninstall the application.

Account and Authentication

Element Burst may use Google Firebase Authentication to identify or authenticate players where online account functionality is enabled.

Depending on the sign-in method used, account-related information may include a Firebase user ID, email address, display name, profile photo URL, authentication provider, account status, and sign-in timestamps.

Authentication data is used to provide account features and associate cloud saves, statistics, and leaderboard records with the correct player.

Firebase and Cloud Firestore

Element Burst may use Google Firebase and Cloud Firestore to store or synchronize limited game and account information needed for online features.

Depending on enabled features, this may include player identifiers, profile details, gameplay statistics, rankings, daily streaks, level completion milestones, reward state, feature flags, and synchronization timestamps.

Player Statistics and Rankings

Element Burst may provide player statistics such as daily streak, levels completed, weekly progress, best streak, total levels completed, win rate, play days, coins earned, personal bests, rank, and percentile.

Global or weekly rankings may use a player identifier, display name, or permitted profile details alongside gameplay metrics. Leaderboards showcase game performance and never expose private account credentials.

Gameplay Telemetry and Analytics

Element Burst may collect gameplay and product telemetry to understand usage trends and improve reliability, game balancing, engagement, and overall player experience.

This telemetry may include app version, platform, session activity, levels started/completed, win/loss events, progress milestones, reward events, ad interactions, error states, and performance metrics.

Telemetry is processed asynchronously in batches to ensure seamless and responsive gameplay.

Administrative Analytics

JRP Apps may use restricted administrative analytics to monitor and maintain Element Burst. These operational metrics are separate from player-facing statistics and are not accessible to regular players.

Administrative analytics include aggregate installation counts, active user metrics, average levels completed, engagement trends, and system health indicators.

Advertising

Element Burst may display advertisements through Google AdMob on supported platforms where advertising is enabled.

Google Mobile Ads may process information such as device/app identifiers, IP address, general location derived from IP, ad interactions, and diagnostic metrics to deliver, measure, secure, and (where permitted) personalize advertising according to your consent settings and Google's privacy policy.

Diagnostics and Technical Information

Element Burst may process diagnostic information to troubleshoot crashes, failed network operations, authentication errors, or performance bottlenecks.

Diagnostic records may include app build version, operating system, runtime characteristics, error logs, and network state.

Permissions and Network Access

Element Burst requests only the permissions necessary for its active features:

  • Network Access (android.permission.INTERNET, android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Required for online features such as Firebase authentication, cloud leaderboards, telemetry, and advertising.
  • Haptic Feedback (android.permission.VIBRATE): Provides tactile feedback during gameplay interactions.

Data Security

Element Burst utilizes the security mechanisms provided by Android, Google Firebase, and HTTPS encryption for information transmitted to and from online services.

While robust technical safeguards are implemented, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission is 100% secure. You are encouraged to maintain the security of your device and credentials.

Data Retention and Deletion

Local game data remains on your device until cleared in device settings or removed by uninstalling the application.

Cloud-hosted account data, statistics, and ranking records are retained for as long as needed to support game services, maintain leaderboard integrity, and fulfill operational or legal requirements. Where in-app account deletion is available, requests will remove or schedule removal of user-owned account records.

Children's Privacy

Element Burst is not directed toward children under applicable age thresholds and does not knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate parental consent.
