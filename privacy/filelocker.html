File Locker Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 3, 2026

File Locker is developed by JRP Apps.

Overview

File Locker is designed to help users securely store and organize files on their own device. The app does not sell personal information and does not use advertising or analytics services.

Information and Files Stored in the App

Files, folders, labels, notes, settings, and related information added to File Locker are stored locally on your device. JRP Apps does not receive, view, or store the contents of your locker.

Encrypted Local Storage

File Locker uses local encryption and device security features to help protect stored content. Your files remain under your control on your device. No security method can guarantee complete protection, so you are responsible for keeping your device, PIN, biometric credentials, and backup files secure.

PIN and Biometric Authentication

File Locker may allow you to protect access using a PIN or biometric authentication supported by your device. Biometric information is processed by your device's operating system. File Locker does not receive or store your fingerprint, face data, or other biometric template.

Local Backup and Restore

File Locker allows you to create local backup files and restore data from backups that you select. Backup files are saved only to the location you choose or shared through options provided by your device. JRP Apps does not receive or store your backup files.

You are responsible for protecting backup files and storing them in a secure location. Anyone who obtains an accessible backup file may be able to attempt to restore or access its contents, depending on the protection applied to that backup.

Permissions

File Locker requests only the permissions required to provide its features. Depending on your device and the action you choose, the app may use system file-selection, storage, camera, or biometric services to import files, save backups, restore data, capture documents, or unlock the app.

Permissions are used only to complete the action you request. You can manage app permissions through your device settings.

Third-Party Services

The current version of File Locker does not use advertising services, analytics services, cloud synchronization, or developer-operated servers for storing your files.

If third-party or cloud services are introduced in a future release, this Privacy Policy will be updated before those services are made available.

Data Sharing

JRP Apps does not sell, rent, or share the contents of your File Locker with third parties. Files are shared only when you choose to export, save, open, or share them using options available on your device.

Data Deletion

You can delete individual files, folders, or other stored information within the app. You can also remove local app data by using the available reset or delete options, clearing the app's storage through device settings, or uninstalling the app.

Local backup files saved outside the app must be deleted separately from the location where you stored them.

Children's Privacy

File Locker is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app stores data locally and does not operate an online account or analytics service, JRP Apps does not knowingly receive children's personal information through the app.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when features, permissions, security methods, or data-handling practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with a revised update date.
