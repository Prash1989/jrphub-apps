Money Mate Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 3, 2026

Money Mate is developed by JRP Apps.

Information Collection

Money Mate is designed to help users manage their personal finances. The current version of the app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal financial information with JRP Apps.

Financial Data

Your income, expenses, budgets, categories, payment methods, notes, and reports are stored locally on your device. JRP Apps does not operate a server that receives or stores this financial information.

Local Backups, Imports, and Exports

Money Mate may allow you to create local backups, restore backup files, import data, or export reports and transaction records. These actions are performed only when you choose to use them.

Backup and export files are saved to a location you select or shared using the options available on your device. JRP Apps does not receive, upload, or store these files.

Cloud Backup and Online Accounts

The current live version of Money Mate does not provide cloud backup, cloud synchronization, or a Money Mate online account. If any such feature is introduced in a future release, this Privacy Policy will be updated before or when that feature becomes available.

Advertising and Analytics

The current live version of Money Mate does not display advertisements and does not use advertising services. It also does not use third-party analytics services to track your activity inside the app.

Permissions

Money Mate requests only the permissions or system access needed to provide its features. For example, the app may use the device file picker, storage access provided by the operating system, or sharing tools when you choose to import, export, back up, restore, open, or share a file.

These permissions and system features are used only to complete the action you requested.

Data Security

Your financial data remains under your control on your device. You are responsible for keeping your device, device passcode, app lock, and any backup files secure.

Although Money Mate is designed to protect locally stored information, no method of electronic storage or device protection can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data Deletion

You can delete individual records from within Money Mate. You can also remove the app's locally stored data by using the available reset or delete options in the app, clearing the app's data through your device settings, or uninstalling the app.

Any local backup or exported files saved outside the app must be deleted separately from the location where you saved them.

Children's Privacy

Money Mate is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third-Party Services

The current live version of Money Mate does not use Google AdMob, cloud backup providers, developer-operated servers, or third-party analytics to process your financial information.

Your device's operating system or apps you choose when opening, saving, or sharing a file may process that file according to their own privacy terms.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Money Mate adds, removes, or changes features, permissions, or third-party services. The updated policy will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
