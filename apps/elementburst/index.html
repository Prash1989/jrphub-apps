Overview

Element Burst brings strategic elemental reactions and pattern clearing into an addictive logic puzzle experience. Players combine fire, water, earth, and air tiles to trigger chain reactions, clear crowded boards, and climb competitive leaderboards.

Features and Why They Matter

  • Elemental Reaction Mechanics. Harness dynamic tile combinations where elements interact according to natural counters and explosive synergies.
  • Global Rankings & Statistics. Compete on leaderboards, review personal match statistics, and track your highest puzzle combos.
  • Cloud Progress Saves. Seamless Firebase cloud integration preserves your unlockable levels, player stats, and achievements across devices.
  • Progressive Challenge Levels. Hundreds of handcrafted elemental boards designed to test tactical foresight and combo planning.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you play Element Burst?

Match and connect complementary elemental tiles to trigger cascading chain reactions and clear target objectives before moves run out.

Can I play Element Burst offline?

Core puzzle levels can be played offline. Cloud save syncing, leaderboard updates, and multiplayer rankings require an internet connection.

Which Android versions are supported?

Element Burst supports Android 10 through Android 16.
