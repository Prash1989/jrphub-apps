Overview

TestCircle is a dedicated developer-to-developer platform created to solve the mandatory 14-day 20 testers requirement for Google Play production access. Instead of struggling to find reliable independent testers, developers join a reciprocal community where creators test each other's apps, provide genuine feedback, and keep testing streaks active.

Features and Why They Matter

  • Guaranteed 20 Testers Community. Connect with verified Android developers and active beta testers ready to install and engage with your app daily.
  • Real-time Testing Streak Monitoring. Track daily active testing progress, countdown to the 14-day production milestone, and verify tester engagement.
  • Valuable Quality & Bug Feedback. Receive actionable crash reports, usability impressions, and device performance metrics before public release.
  • Reciprocal Community Credits. Earn testing points by helping other creators test their applications, keeping the entire platform free and supportive.
  • Cross-Platform Android & Web Access. Manage applications, review testing logs, and engage with testers from your Android phone or desktop browser.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is TestCircle designed for?

TestCircle helps indie Android developers and teams meet Google Play's 20 testers for 14 continuous days closed testing requirement through an active, collaborative developer community.

Is TestCircle available on both Play Store and Web?

Yes. TestCircle offers a native Android app on Google Play as well as an interactive web portal at testcircle.jrphub.com.

How does reciprocal testing work?

Developers test other community members' apps to earn testing credits, which ensures active participation and reciprocal testing for their own releases.

Does TestCircle support bug reporting?

Yes, testers can submit bug details, device logs, and constructive feedback directly to app owners.
