Voice Mate

A private, local-first voice recorder for capturing crystal-clear audio notes, meetings, lectures, and ideas with complete privacy and zero cloud dependence.

Join Testing Track ↗ Google Play Listing ↗ Contact support

Overview

Voice Mate solves the challenge of capturing important moments, thoughts, and conversations without compromising privacy or relying on third-party cloud services. Designed from the ground up as a private, offline-first audio recorder, Voice Mate ensures your voice notes, lectures, interviews, and confidential meetings remain strictly under your control.

Built for fluid, immediate use, Voice Mate lets you start recording in a single tap with real-time waveform visualization and pause/resume capabilities. Your recordings are automatically indexed with smart timestamps, editable titles, custom folders, and favorite flags for effortless retrieval.

Voice Mate pairs seamlessly with other focused tools in the JRP ecosystem. Review expense voice notes alongside Money Mate, draft related thoughts with DocsStudio, or use Phone Toolbox for hardware checks and sensor tools.

Features and Why They Matter

  • High-Fidelity Audio Capture. Record crystal-clear voice notes, lectures, interviews, and memos with real-time live waveform visualization and pause/resume controls.
  • 100% Private & Offline-First. Recordings stay entirely on your device storage or browser CacheStorage. No user accounts, no backend servers, no cloud sync, and zero tracking or telemetry.
  • Folder Management & Tagging. Organize recordings into custom category folders, mark essential clips as favorites, and quickly declutter your audio library.
  • Instant Search & Multi-Mode Sorting. Find any note in seconds with real-time search across titles and folders, plus 6 sort options: newest, oldest, name (A–Z/Z–A), size, and duration.
  • Advanced Audio Player. Enjoy smooth playback with a scrubbable waveform seek bar, quick ±10 second skip buttons, and multi-speed playback (0.75x, 1.0x, 1.25x, 1.5x, 2.0x).
  • Native Export & Sharing. Share audio files directly through standard Android system sharing, Bluetooth, messaging, or email without cloud intermediaries.

Who Should Use It

Voice Mate is crafted for anyone who values clarity, convenience, and absolute confidentiality in voice recording:

  • Students & Academics: Record full lectures, study group discussions, and research notes, then review them quickly with 1.5x or 2.0x playback speeds.
  • Professionals & Freelancers: Keep confidential records of client consultations, brainstorming sessions, and project meetings without exposing audio to third-party servers.
  • Journalists & Writers: Capture high-quality field interviews, quick voice memos, and spontaneous story ideas with timestamped accuracy.
  • Everyday Users: Record personal reminders, voice diaries, musical ideas, and grocery lists with an intuitive, clutter-free interface.

Privacy-First Data Handling

Your voice recordings can reveal intimate thoughts, private conversations, and sensitive details. Voice Mate was engineered from day one with a strict local-first philosophy:

The app requires only the microphone permission to capture audio while actively recording. It does not record in the background without user knowledge, requires no sign-up or profile, and contains zero third-party telemetry, tracking libraries, or analytics SDKs.

For complete details on data handling and device permissions, please read our dedicated Voice Mate Privacy Policy.

Fast, Lightweight Android Performance

Voice Mate is engineered for instant readiness. When you need to capture a fleeting thought or an urgent conversation, the app launches instantly without splash delays or network dependencies.

The interface follows clean Material Design principles with accessible touch targets, responsive waveforms, dark/light theme support, and battery-friendly audio codecs optimized across Android 10 through Android 16.

Offline Use and Cross-Platform Web Support

All core functionalities—recording, playback, folder organization, search, and local export—are 100% functional offline. Additionally, Voice Mate features a responsive web edition powered by the HTML5 Web Audio API and local browser CacheStorage, allowing secure on-device recording across desktop and mobile browsers.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Voice Mate used for?

Voice Mate is used for capturing audio notes, lectures, interviews, meetings, and ideas with complete on-device privacy.

Does Voice Mate send recordings to a cloud server?

No. Voice Mate operates entirely locally on your device. There is no remote backend, no user account, and zero cloud synchronization.

Can I organize my voice notes into folders?

Yes. You can create custom folders, bookmark favorites, and sort recordings by date, name, file size, or duration.

Can I adjust playback speed?

Yes. The built-in player provides variable playback speeds from 0.75x to 2.0x, along with ±10 second skip buttons and scrub controls.

Which Android versions are supported?

Voice Mate supports Android 10 through the latest Android 16 (API 23+) and is also accessible via modern web browsers.
