Family Docs

Family document management, encrypted offline storage, category tagging, and OCR document scanning on Android.

Join Testing Track ↗ Google Play Listing ↗ Support

Overview

Family Docs is a secure, encrypted organizer built to help families scan, organize, and safeguard essential personal records—from health cards, IDs, and insurance policies to warranties and educational certificates.

Features and Why They Matter

  • AES-256 On-Device Encryption. Sensitive family records are encrypted locally before being stored, ensuring zero unauthorized access.
  • Smart OCR Document Scanning. Capture documents with your camera, automatically crop edges, and extract searchable text with built-in OCR.
  • Member & Category Organization. Organize documents by family member (Parents, Children) and categories (Medical, Identity, Vehicles, Finance).
  • Expiry & Renewal Reminders. Set reminder alerts for upcoming passport, visa, license, or insurance policy renewals.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are my family documents uploaded to a cloud server?

No. Family Docs is an encrypted offline-first app. Documents remain securely encrypted on your local device storage.

How does OCR document scanning work?

The app processes document images on-device to recognize text and enhance contrast for archival quality.
