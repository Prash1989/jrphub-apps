PMS (Powerloom Management System)

Overview

PMS (Powerloom Management System) is an enterprise production and inventory management tool tailored specifically for textile weaving factories and powerloom organizations. It replaces paper logbooks with real-time tracking of loom RPM, shift-wise cloth production, warp beam inventory, weaver attendance, and maintenance cycles.

Features and Why They Matter

  • Loom Operations & Shift Tracking. Monitor active looms, shift-wise cloth yardage/meters produced, and machine downtime directly on the factory floor.
  • Warp Beam & Yarn Inventory. Manage beam stock, track remaining warp length, calculate wastage percentages, and forecast raw material replenishment.
  • Weaver & Operator Logs. Record daily operator shifts, calculate piece-rate wages, and track individual weaver efficiency.
  • Offline-First Factory Sync. Works seamlessly in factory environments with intermittent internet, syncing production records once connectivity is restored.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is PMS used for?

PMS is designed for powerloom textile units to manage daily weaving operations, beam inventory, shift production logs, and worker productivity.

Can PMS operate offline?

Yes, floor managers can enter shift production and loom logs completely offline. Data synchronizes automatically when online.

Who can use PMS?

Weaving mill owners, powerloom shed managers, supervisors, and textile manufacturers.
